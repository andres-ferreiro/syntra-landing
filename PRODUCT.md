## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4) — inherited from the existing `create-next-app` scaffold in this repo.

## Users

Business owners and marketers considering an AI-powered automation/CRM buildout for their business (local services, clinics, sales teams, and similar lead-driven businesses are a typical fit for GoHighLevel-based agencies). They land on this page evaluating whether to inquire about a done-for-you automation setup.

## Product Purpose

mandhy is a whitelabel automation agency: it builds AI-powered chatbots connected to a client's CRM, scheduling, custom automations, and Google reputation/review management — all tailored to drive conversion for the client's business. The underlying platform is whitelabeled and must never be named (no "GoHighLevel," "GHL," or other underlying vendor/platform names anywhere in copy, code, comments, or assets) — mandhy presents the stack as its own. This landing page's job is to explain that offering and convert visitors into inquiries.

## Positioning

Not a generic "AI chatbot" or "marketing agency" — mandhy's mechanism is a fully connected, proprietary-presented automation stack (chatbot + CRM + scheduling + custom automations + reputation management) built specifically to drive conversion, not just engagement. The platform underneath is whitelabeled; mandhy is the only brand ever surfaced.

## Operating Context

The primary conversion action is a contact/inquiry form (or equivalent lead-capture path) — visitors are meant to reach out to start a conversation about a tailored automation buildout.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Core service components to represent: AI-powered chatbots, CRM integration, scheduling, custom automations, Google reputation/review management.
- Everything described as "tailored for conversion" — the page should carry that conversion-first framing rather than treating these as generic feature bullets.
- Hard constraint: whitelabel. Never mention GoHighLevel, GHL, or any underlying platform/vendor name in copy, metadata, code comments, filenames, or assets. mandhy is always presented as the sole brand behind the stack.
- No pricing, packages, or case studies confirmed yet — do not fabricate these.

## Brand Commitments

Whitelabel: mandhy is the only brand ever surfaced. The underlying automation platform must never be named or hinted at (no "GoHighLevel"/"GHL" or equivalent). Logo: `public/images/mandhy-logo.png` (full wordmark) and `public/images/mandhy-icon.png` (standalone "m." mark, used for favicons/icons) — see DESIGN.md.

## Evidence on Hand

## Product Principles

1. Lead with conversion outcomes (more booked appointments, more replies, better reviews), not raw feature lists.
2. Make the "connected stack" mechanism (chatbot ↔ CRM ↔ scheduling ↔ automations ↔ reputation) legible as one system, not five disconnected tools.
3. Keep the primary CTA (inquiry/contact) unmissable and repeated at natural decision points.
4. Never invent client proof (logos, testimonials, stats) — earn trust through clarity of offer instead until real evidence exists.
