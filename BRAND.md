# Brand

## Positioning

mandhy is an all-in-one AI assistant for your business — not a system you have to operate. It answers customers, books calls, follows up on every lead, keeps the CRM organized, and asks happy customers for reviews, so a business runs as if it had a full team behind it.

Differentiator: most competitors sell "automation" or "a connected system" — infrastructure the business has to configure and run. mandhy is sold and written about as an assistant that already does the work.

Audience: business owners and marketers considering an AI-powered automation/CRM buildout (local services, clinics, sales teams, and similar lead-driven businesses).

## Personality & Voice

mandhy reads as an active, capable assistant — not infrastructure. Concretely:

1. **mandhy is the subject of the sentence, not "the system."** Write what mandhy does (answers, books, follows up), not what gets connected or configured.
2. **Formal register.** Second person ("your business"), active voice, confident and calm. No slang, no exclamation-point hype, no salesy filler.
3. **Brand name always lowercase** — "mandhy," including sentence-initial position.
4. **Third-person point of view** everywhere except the chat-mockup component, where mandhy's replies are naturally first-person as a chat-UI convention.
5. **The business's pain stays about the business, not personified.** Problem-statement copy ("leads get lost," "no-shows pile up") describes what's broken before mandhy enters the picture — don't put mandhy in sentences about the problem.

## Vocabulary

**Lean into**: answers, books, follows up, reminds, organizes, notices, never forgets, handles, keeps track of.

**Retire from headline/lead copy**: "system," "platform," "workflows," "pipeline," "integrations." These stay fine in precise/technical detail (FAQ specifics, form fields) but shouldn't drive the top-level narrative.

## Before / After

- **Hero.** Before: "mandhy connects your website, CRM, messaging, follow-up, appointments, and reputation into one system built around how your business actually works." After: "mandhy answers your customers, books their calls, follows up on every lead, and keeps your reputation growing — so your business runs like it has a full team behind it."
- **Feature (CRM).** Before: "A clear pipeline of contacts and opportunities, no more scattered spreadsheets." After: "mandhy keeps every contact and opportunity organized in a clear pipeline — no more scattered spreadsheets."
- **FAQ.** Before: "No. Syntra automates repetitive work and uses AI for speed, but important decisions... always go through a person." After: "No. mandhy handles the repetitive work and responds at AI speed, but the decisions that matter — negotiation, complaints, sensitive cases — always go to a person."
- **Closing CTA.** Before: "Your business does not need more manual work. It needs better systems." After: "Your business doesn't need more manual work. It needs mandhy."

## Hard Constraints (unchanged from PRODUCT.md)

Whitelabel: mandhy is the only brand ever surfaced. The underlying automation platform must never be named or hinted at (no "GoHighLevel"/"GHL" or equivalent), in copy, code, or assets.
